Yamabiko

A private app for keeping track of my own hikes.

Yamabiko is a personal, self-hosted fork of Endurain. It runs on my own server and has one user: me. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is no public sign-up.

It imports my workouts from Google Health (my Fitbit) and from files, and shows them as maps, charts, times on the stretches I walk often, and a map of where I have been.

The source code is public: github.com/fredrikudengen/yamabiko.
